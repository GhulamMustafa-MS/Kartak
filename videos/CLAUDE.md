# videos/

Short clips the worker app shows (served as they are; not saved for offline use, to keep the first visit small).

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `cross-contamination.mp4` | 13 s cartoon (1280x720, H.264, no sound): a carrot cut on a green board, the slices moved into a bowl, then raw chicken cut on the same board without cleaning it. Used by the "Key words" card w06 (`data/words.json`, D27) |

Rules: keep clips short (under 1 MB) and without sound or words, so they work in every language. Made by drawing
each frame with code and joining them with ffmpeg (the method is in docs/plan.md "Make a clip").
